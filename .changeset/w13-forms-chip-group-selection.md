`lr-chip-group` gains an opt-in selection model: `selection="single|multiple"`, a `value` (string, or an array in `multiple`), an `lr-change` event, and `all-value` to name an "all" chip that clears the selection and cannot be un-pressed. With `selection` unset nothing changes.
