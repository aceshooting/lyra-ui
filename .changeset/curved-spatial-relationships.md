Add signed curvature for 3D graph edges, including typed columns, provider scenes, built-in rendering and the optional WebGL companion. Curved paths retain edge identities and support continuous dashes, tangent arrows, midpoint labels and curve-aware fitting. Existing straight edges and 2D behavior remain unchanged.

The curved-edge renderer and guides measure 147,026 packed and 601,579 unpacked companion bytes. The affected archive limits advance only to the next 64-byte boundary.
