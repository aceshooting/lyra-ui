The dashboard chart roles `bar-list` and `category-bar` now render the dedicated `lr-bar-list` and `lr-category-bar` components with plain-data schemas (`items`/`sort`/`max`, and `values`/`variants`/`marker`/`withLabels`), and a new `radial-bar` role renders concentric progress rings through `lr-radial-bar`. The polar-area `radial-chart` role is unchanged. The `tracker` role now requires a `label`, used as the strip's accessible name.
