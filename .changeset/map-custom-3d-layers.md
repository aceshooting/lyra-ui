Add `addMapCustomLayer(map, layer, options?)` from the new `@aceshooting/lyra-ui/map-custom-layers.js` entry for trusted custom WebGL2 layers on `lr-map`, such as beams, coverage cones, clouds or particles. A layer's `create(context)` receives the map's own WebGL2 context and geographic services; each frame provides the shared camera as a Mercator/globe shader prelude with projection uniforms, tile-local encoding, CSS projection and sea-level or terrain-relative altitude resolution. Layers are reinstalled with a fresh instance after a `mapStyle` replacement and after WebGL context restoration, disposed on `remove()` or map disconnection, and report `loading`, `ready`, `unavailable` and `removed` states. A pointer click that the layer's `pick()` hits emits `lr-map-scene-activate` with `kind: 'custom'` and `layerId`. The bridge loads from the optional `@aceshooting/lyra-map/scene` entry on first use; ordinary `lr-map` imports do not load it.

Scene paths and arcs with a nonzero altitude, a nonzero arc `height` or `sea-level` geometry over displayed terrain are now drawn in 3D at their authored altitude through that bridge, so the scene reports `ready` instead of `partial` with code `altitude`. Lines keep their CSS pixel width, depth-test against the map and hide behind the globe horizon; `terrain` altitude adds displayed terrain height, blending linearly along a segment between `terrain` and `sea-level` endpoints. When the bridge is unavailable or fails, those lines keep the ground-track fallback and the `partial`/`altitude` report.
