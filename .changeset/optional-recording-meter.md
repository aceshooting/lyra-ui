Keep push-to-talk recording active when its optional audio-level analysis cannot initialize, including when level events are enabled during capture. Close partially initialized audio contexts without reporting a recorder failure or rejecting the component update; microphone capture and normal stop behavior remain available.

The optional-analysis guard adds 7–10 gzip bytes to five complete or conversation entry bundles. Their previously exact ceilings advance only to the reviewed measurements, with no extra allowance; passing ceilings and optional-peer exclusions remain unchanged.
