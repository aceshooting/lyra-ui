Discard obsolete contact-file responses before reading their bodies when the viewer disconnects or its source changes while response headers are pending. Late network failures and aborts also remain isolated from the current document across the viewer family.

The guard adds 58 runtime bytes to the unchanged package inventory. Only the exceeded unpacked-package and two aggregate gzip ceilings advance to their next 64-byte boundaries after measurement.
