- A `focus()` call made before a component's first render now lands once it renders, across the remaining delegating controls: agent tools, conversation, data, layout, media, overlays, utility and the checkbox, radio and toggle groups. A host that is disabled or removed by then stays unfocused.
- `lr-tab` also waits for its group to render the real tab button before focusing it.
