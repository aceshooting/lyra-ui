`lr-table` gains opt-in structure controls. `stack-header` (`stackHeader: 'visible' | 'sortable' | 'hidden'`, default `'visible'`) chooses what the column header strip shows while rows are stacked: `'sortable'` keeps only the sortable headers as a compact sort strip and `'hidden'` keeps none. Headers left out stay in the accessibility tree and are shown while focused. A column's `rowHeader: true` renders its body cells as `<th scope="row" role="rowheader">`, styled like the row and tunable through `--lr-table-row-header-font-weight`. `without-header-row` (`withoutHeaderRow`) omits the `<thead>` for a header-less table named by its `caption` or `aria-label`. `rowAttributes(row, index)` adds `data-*` attributes and `aria-label`/`aria-description`/`aria-current`/`aria-rowindex` to each body row, and drops event handlers, `style` and every other name. A development build now warns when a `cellPart` result reuses one of the table's own part names, such as `cell-label`. A column's `wrap: 'wrap' | 'nowrap'` sets line wrapping for its body and footer cells (unset keeps today's wrapping; a `'nowrap'` column wraps while stacked). New tokens, each defaulting to today's rendering: `--lr-table-border` (the frame border shorthand), `--lr-table-cell-line-height` (body cells) and `--lr-table-sort-icon-vertical-align` (`middle`). New exported types: `TableStackHeader`, `TableRowAttributes`, `TableRowAttributeValue` and `TableColumnWrap`.
