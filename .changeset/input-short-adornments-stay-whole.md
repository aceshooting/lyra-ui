A short `start`/`end` adornment such as a measurement unit now stays whole in a narrow `lr-input`, `lr-number-input`, `lr-native-time-input` or `lr-date-input`. Before, the native field's flex basis came from its `size`-derived intrinsic width, not from its value, so in a 210px `lr-input type="number"` holding `1650` the field kept most of the row while a `kWh` end unit was clipped, and `lr-number-input`'s steppers left even less room for the unit. The native field now gives up its spare width first, and while an adornment is shown it keeps a four-character floor; only then do the adornments shrink. A genuinely long adornment is still capped (at half the row in `lr-input`, 40% in `lr-date-input`) and still ellipsizes. `lr-date-input` adornments are now sized to their content instead of reserving 40% of the row, so a three-letter adornment no longer leaves a wide gap in a wide field. Shrink-to-fit containers still size the field to its native intrinsic width. A row without adornments is unchanged: with no floor, its field still yields entirely to the clear, password-toggle, stepper and calendar actions. A row that shows an adornment but is narrower than its fixed actions plus the floor now overflows instead of collapsing the field to nothing. A `flex-basis: 0` override on `::part(input)` is no longer needed. There is no new option or token.
