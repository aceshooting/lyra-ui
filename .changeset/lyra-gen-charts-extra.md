New reviewed chart roles exported as `chartExtraRoles` broaden the dashboard vocabulary: `donut-chart` (`lr-doughnut-chart`), `radial-chart` (`lr-polar-area-chart`), `radar-chart` (`lr-radar-chart`), `horizontal-bar` and `bar-list` (`lr-bar-chart` profiles whose horizontal axis, and for the ranked list the hidden legend and grid, are pinned by a trusted adapter), `bubble-chart` (`lr-bubble-chart`), `category-bar` (`lr-context-meter` segments with variant tones), `tracker` (`lr-sequence-strip` cells coloured only from a closed set of theme colour tokens) and `chord` (`lr-chord`). Every role renders from plain bounded JSON with a usage guide and one reviewed example; raw colours, formatters, plugins, chart configuration and activation callbacks stay host-owned.
