Form labels take `--lr-form-control-label-text-align`, `--lr-form-control-label-text-transform` and `--lr-form-control-label-block-gap` (the gap between a label above its field and the field, also in a collapsed `label-placement="start"` row). `lr-input` adds `--lr-input-line-height` and `--lr-input-number-direction` for numeric content; `lr-date-input` adds `--lr-date-input-action-color` for the resting clear and calendar actions; `lr-slider` adds `--lr-slider-thumb-border-width`, `--lr-slider-thumb-shadow`, `--lr-slider-value-color` and `--lr-slider-value-font-family`; `lr-locale-picker` adds `--lr-locale-picker-listbox-min-inline-size`; `lr-button` adds `--lr-button-line-height`, `--lr-button-link-text-decoration`, `--lr-button-link-hover-text-decoration` and `--lr-button-link-min-height`. Every default is unchanged.
