Adds opt-in typography styles to `utilities.css`: `lr-heading-1`–`lr-heading-4` and `lr-inline-code` role classes, an `lr-typography` scope that styles headings, paragraphs, links, blockquotes, lists, inline code and tables inside it, and an `lr-not-typography` boundary to opt a subtree out.
