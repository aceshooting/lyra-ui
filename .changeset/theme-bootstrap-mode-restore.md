Add mode-only prepaint restoration through `createLyraThemeBootstrap({ restore: 'mode' })` and the classic script's `data-lr-theme-restore="mode"` attribute. Hosts can keep fixed look, surface, density, accent, and inline styles while restoring saved light/dark/System mode without importing the full style runtime. Whole-profile restoration remains the default.
