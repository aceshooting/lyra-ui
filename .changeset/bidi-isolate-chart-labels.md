Formatted chart labels now keep their own reading order inside `dir="rtl"`. Before, a number-first label such as `2.4 MiB/s`, `9:00 AM` or `-3` took on the chart's right-to-left direction and painted unit-first (`MiB/s 2.4`), even though the axis itself was placed correctly. Each label now paints in the direction of its own first strong character, like `<bdi>`, so an Arabic- or Hebrew-script label keeps its right-to-left order too. This covers `lr-lite-chart` value-axis ticks, category ticks, axis titles, legend values, generated table cells and the visible data list; `lr-chart`, its typed subclasses, `lr-histogram` and `lr-box-plot` tick labels, axis titles, tooltips (including a formatted value that follows an Arabic- or Hebrew-script series name), data labels, annotation labels, legend values and table cells; `lr-gauge` captions; and `lr-heatmap`'s tooltip and legend scale labels. Axis placement, category order and label fitting are unchanged, and a left-to-right chart with left-to-right labels draws exactly the same strings. Accessible names, announcements and CSV export gain no bidi control characters. No text-direction option is needed, and formatters should keep returning plain text.
