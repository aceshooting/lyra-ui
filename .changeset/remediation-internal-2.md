Optional-peer loaders can word the development warning differently for a load failure than for a missing package. Remote response buffering sizes its buffer once from a trusted Content-Length instead of growing and copying. Icon-button hover and press now mix from a themed resting fill and stay visible under forced colors, as do progress tracks and fills. Form-control hint and error text wrap inside narrow controls, and SSR fallback attributes escape single quotes.
