# Code of conduct

We want Lyra UI participation to be welcoming, respectful and safe for everyone, regardless of
age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, religion,
sexual orientation, or experience level.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code respectfully, accept good-faith feedback,
respect personal boundaries, and help others participate. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks
are not.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexual advances or imagery, intimidation, publishing
someone's private information without permission, and retaliation against a reporter are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
It also applies when someone represents Lyra UI publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [info@aceshooting.com](mailto:info@aceshooting.com). Include
what happened, where it happened, and any relevant evidence. Do not post private reports publicly.
The reporting contact will handle reports discreetly, share information only as needed to investigate
and respond, and consider the safety and wishes of affected people. A report may request review by
an uninvolved maintainer if it concerns someone involved in handling reports.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request a change in behavior, issue a warning, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation, proportionate to the behavior and its impact. They will
explain an enforcement decision privately to affected participants when safe and practicable.
Reports made in good faith will not lead to retaliation.
